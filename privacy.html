Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information rushitrades.com (the “Site”) and Rushi Trades (“we”, “us”) collect and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, message us on Telegram, or join our mailing list, we receive what you share, such as your name, email address and message, and use it to reply and provide support.
  • Purchase information. Purchases are handled through third-party checkout and payment providers. We receive the details needed to deliver your access, such as your name, email and TradingView username. We do not store your card details.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting and the third-party services below may log your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

The Site itself does not have a login or account system, and we do not run our own advertising trackers.

2. Third-party content on the Site

The Site loads content from other services, including Google Fonts, a TradingView market ticker and a Myfxbook performance widget. These providers may collect technical data and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

3. How we use information

  • To deliver products and access you purchased
  • To answer questions and provide support
  • To send updates or offers by email, where you have joined our list. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the Site secure and understand how it is used

4. Email services

We may use email service providers, such as Brevo, to send messages. They process your email address on our behalf.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business (such as payment, email and hosting providers) or when the law requires it.

6. Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or to meet legal requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no online service can be completely secure.

7. Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from emails, by writing to us. Where applicable law, such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, gives you additional rights, we will honour them.

8. Children

Our products are intended for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions or requests? Email support@rushitrades.com.
