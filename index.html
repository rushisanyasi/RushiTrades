Questions, answered

What do your indicators do?

Elite Signal plots buy and sell setups on your TradingView chart with take profit and stop loss already marked. Institutional Zones is free and maps supply, demand and key reversal zones. Elite Alpha adds non-repaint entry limits in advance, with real-time alerts.

Which markets and timeframes can I use them on?

They are built for XAUUSD and index traders. Members also use Elite Signal on NIFTY, midcap futures and forex, often on the 1-minute and 5-minute charts. Test on the market and timeframe you plan to trade before using real money.

Does it guarantee profit?

No indicator can. It shows setups and defines your risk, but results still depend on your position size, discipline and risk management. Losing trades are a normal part of trading.

I have a job. Can I use it without watching charts all day?

Many members do. The idea is to wait for a signal, check the setup, place your SL and TP, and step away. Ask on Telegram how to set up alerts for the indicator you use.

Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

The indicators run on TradingView. A free account limits how many indicators you can put on one chart, so check TradingView’s current plan limits for your setup.

How do I get access?

Use the “Access institutional tools” button at the top of this page. Institutional Zones is free. If you have any trouble with payment or access, message me on Telegram or email support@rushitrades.com.

Can I use it on a funded or prop firm account?

Some members do. Read your prop firm’s rules first, and size each trade so a single loss can’t break the daily or total drawdown limit.

What are your algos?

MT5 expert advisors built mainly for XAUUSD: Elite Alpha Bot (the flagship), Elite Grid Bot, Elite Nano Grid Bot and Elite HFT Grid Bot.

Is the performance real?

The live widget in the Algos section comes from my Myfxbook account. Click it to see the full record. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Will it make money every month?

No. Algos go through drawdowns and results vary from month to month. Judge it over a longer period, not a few days, and only with risk you can afford.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

MT5 bots only trade while MT5 is running. Most users run them on a VPS so trading continues when their own computer is off.

How much capital do I need?

It depends on the bot, lot size and your broker. Start small, never risk money you can’t afford to lose, and ask on Telegram before choosing a size.

Can I run it on a prop firm account?

Prop firm rules differ, and some limit expert advisors, grid or high-frequency strategies. Read your firm’s rules before using any bot, especially a grid or HFT one.

I live in India. Can I trade forex or gold CFDs?

Rules for residents of India under RBI and FEMA are strict. This is not legal advice. Read the FEMA disclaimer in the footer and check with a qualified professional before trading on any offshore platform.
